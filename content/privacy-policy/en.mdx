# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this website (the "Site") collects, uses, and protects personal information.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to data processed through this Site, including:
- browsing Site pages;
- subscribing to newsletter emails;
- submitting product URLs through Site forms; and
- interacting with analytics and advertising scripts enabled on the Site.

It does not govern third-party websites, products, or services linked from this Site.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide directly

We may collect:
- email address (newsletter subscription and unsubscribe processing);
- product URL you submit via the product submission form; and
- messages or contact information you choose to provide through available contact channels.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

We may collect:
- IP address or proxy-forwarded IP (for abuse prevention/rate limiting);
- device/browser data, referring URL, pages visited, and basic event metadata;
- cookie data such as locale preference (for example, `NEXT_LOCALE`); and
- analytics and ad-related identifiers when analytics/ads are enabled.

### 2.3 Operational and security data

We may generate server-side operational records such as:
- request logs;
- rate-limiting records; and
- error diagnostics.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- provide and improve Site features and content;
- deliver newsletter emails and process unsubscribe requests;
- operate anti-abuse/rate-limiting controls;
- measure traffic and performance;
- support advertising integrations (if enabled); and
- meet legal, compliance, and security obligations.

## 4. Legal Bases (Where Required)

Depending on your region, we process data under one or more legal bases:
- consent (for example, optional subscriptions or optional analytics/ads where required);
- legitimate interests (Site operation, security, fraud prevention, and performance measurement);
- contractual necessity (to fulfill requested services); and
- legal obligations.

## 5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies for:
- core functionality (such as language preference);
- analytics (for example, Google Analytics, Plausible, Baidu Tongji, and Vercel Analytics if configured); and
- advertising (for example, Google AdSense if configured).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect Site functionality.

## 6. Sharing and Third-Party Processing

We may share data with service providers that help us operate the Site, including:
- email delivery/contact management providers (for newsletter operations);
- infrastructure, hosting, and observability providers;
- analytics providers; and
- advertising providers (if enabled).

We may also disclose data when required by law or to protect rights, security, and platform integrity.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Because service providers may operate globally, your data may be processed in countries outside your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards under applicable law.

## 8. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, including:
- newsletter data: until you unsubscribe or request deletion, subject to legal/operational needs;
- submission form data: for review, moderation, and related operations;
- rate-limit and security records: typically short-term retention;
- logs and diagnostics: short operational retention unless longer storage is required for security or legal reasons.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:
- access, correct, or delete personal information;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis;
- request portability; and
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

For California residents, rights may include the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale/sharing where applicable, and the right to non-discrimination under the CCPA/CPRA.

For EU/EEA/UK users, rights may include those under GDPR/UK GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and complaint rights.

To exercise rights, use the contact methods shown in the Site footer or other official Site contact channels.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

This Site is not intended for children under 13 (or higher age thresholds required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law.

## 11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "lastUpdated" date on this page.

## 13. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact us via the official channels listed in the Site footer.
